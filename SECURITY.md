# Security Policy

## Reporting a Vulnerability

The RoboLayer team takes security seriously. If you discover a vulnerability, please report it responsibly:

📧 **security@robolayer.xyz**

Include:
- Description of the issue
- Steps to reproduce
- Potential impact
- Suggested fix (if any)

**Please do not open public GitHub issues for security vulnerabilities.**

## Response Timeline

- **Acknowledgement**: within 48 hours
- **Initial assessment**: within 5 business days
- **Fix and disclosure**: depends on severity, typically 14–90 days

## Bug Bounty

For critical findings affecting funds at risk on mainnet, RoboLayer offers a bounty program. Severity tiers:

| Severity | Reward (USD) |
|---------|--------------|
| Critical (funds drainable) | up to $50,000 |
| High (significant impact) | up to $10,000 |
| Medium (limited impact) | up to $2,500 |
| Low (best practices) | up to $500 |

Bounty paid in $ROBO or USDC.
